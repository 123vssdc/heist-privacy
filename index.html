HEIST: Steal & Escape — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

HEIST: Steal & Escape does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Your progress (career stats and best score) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you reset your career in the game or delete the app.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos.
