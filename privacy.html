1. Who we are

The controller and developer of "HEIST: Steal & Escape" (the "Game") is an individual, Vadym Maherko (the "Developer"), Ukraine. Privacy contact: vvvadim.businesss@gmail.com.

2. Summary

The Game does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, sign-up, ads, analytics, trackers or third-party SDKs. The Game works offline and makes no network requests.

DataWhereWhy

4. Device permissions

The Game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, tracking (ATT), notifications or Bluetooth access. It only uses haptic feedback where supported.

5. Data processed by Apple

The Game is distributed through the App Store, operated by Apple, which processes data under its own policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). If you enable "Share With App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple may give the Developer aggregated, anonymous download and crash statistics. The Developer receives no personal data from this.

6. Sharing and international transfers

Because no data is collected, none is shared with third parties or transferred across borders.

7. Children

The Game collects no data from anyone, including children under 13 (COPPA, US) or under 16 (GDPR, EU). App Store age rating: 12+ (cartoon/fantasy violence and theft theme, no realistic violence).

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and Ukrainian law you may ask what data is held about you, and request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since the Developer holds no data about you, you can delete everything yourself: use "Reset Career" in the main menu, or delete the app. You may also email requests to the address above; I respond within 30 days. Complaints: the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights (ombudsman.gov.ua) or your local EU/EEA/UK authority.

9. Security

Local data is protected by iOS (app sandbox, device encryption when a passcode is set). Setting a device passcode is recommended.

10. Changes

If the Game ever starts collecting data (e.g. online play, ads or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released and the date above changed.

11. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of Ukraine, without prejudice to mandatory consumer and data-protection rules of your country of residence.
